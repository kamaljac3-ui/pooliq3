01 — GRAINS

A quarter-million grains.

Every grain is a position and a velocity stored in a texture, updated by a shader each frame. The sand you just touched never touches the CPU.

02 — THE CORE

One mesh, shaped by noise.

A plain icosahedron whose vertices ride a 3D simplex field, faceted in the fragment shader and lit only by a rim glow.

03 — FLIGHT

Scroll is the camera.

Your scroll position is a number between 0 and 1. That number walks a spline for the camera and another for where it looks.

04 — PASSAGE

Through the knot.

A torus knot, a dust trail seeded along the flight path, and bloom that only the brightest things in the frame bleed into.

05 — PORTAL

Your scene goes here.

Stage 2 of a build. Swap the core for a product, a landscape, a character — the pipeline stays the same.
